## 5. Lumbee Quick Briefing (so you're fluent on the call)
- ~55,000 enrolled members — largest tribe east of the Mississippi. Centered in Robeson, Hoke, Cumberland, Scotland counties, NC; headquarters in Pembroke ("the Turtle" building).
- Name comes from the Lumbee (Lumber) River. State-recognized since 1885.
- **Just achieved full federal recognition** via the Lumbee Fairness Act in the 2026 NDAA (signed Dec 18, 2025; added to the federal list early 2026) — after ~137 years of advocacy. Current chairman: John Lowery.
- Heritage points of pride: Henry Berry Lowry (folk hero), 1958 Battle of Hayes Pond (Lumbee routed a KKK rally).
- Arvis himself: enrolled Lumbee member, taught/served Native communities (Lumbee, Cherokee, Sioux, First Nations), translated hymns into the Cheraw dialect, longtime advocate for the federal recognition that just happened. Lives in the NC Smokies with wife Kim.
